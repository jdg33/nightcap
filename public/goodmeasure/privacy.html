Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

The short version: Good Measure doesn't show ads, doesn't track you, and doesn't sell anything. Your recipes, logs, and favorites live on your device and in your own iCloud. Something only becomes public if you choose "Share publicly."

Good Measure ("the app") is a cocktail recipe and drink-log app made by Jesse Goldstein ("I", "me"). Its cocktail library comes from my site, nightcap.me. Questions about this policy? Email jesse.d.goldstein@gmail.com.

What the app collects

  • Your Apple sign-in. You sign in with Apple. The app receives a private identifier Apple creates for you and this app, and, the first time, the name you choose to share with it. It never sees your Apple ID password. You can edit the display name shown on anything you make public.
  • What you create. Cocktails you build (name, ingredients, instructions, description, optional photo), drinks you log (drink, date, note, star rating, serving size), favorites, the ingredients in "My Bar," and tags.
  • Your reports. If you report content, I store which item you reported, the reason and any details you typed, and an anonymous code derived from your Apple identifier so one person can't flood the system.

The app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and contains no third-party tracking code.

Where your information lives

  • On your device and in your iCloud. Your recipes, logs, favorites, bar, and tags are saved on your device and synced between your own devices using Apple's iCloud (CloudKit, private database). That copy is private to your Apple account and is governed by Apple's privacy practices.
  • Supabase. The cocktail library (including cocktails people have shared to it) and the reports table are hosted on Supabase.
  • Apple iCloud, public database. Anything you share publicly to Community is stored there.

What becomes public

Only things you switch "Share publicly" on for:

  • A cocktail: its name, ingredients, instructions, description, and your display name (or the author name you typed) appear to everyone in the Community tab. It is also added to the Good Measure cocktail library, which is shown on nightcap.me and in the app for everyone, with the author name you entered. Photos are shown in Community only, not in the library.
  • A drink log ("cheer"): the drink, date, your note, your star rating, and your display name appear in the Community feed and on that drink's page.

Private cocktails and logs are never shared. You can switch a cocktail or log back to private, or delete it, at any time; shared copies are then removed from Community and the library.

How I use it

Only to run the app: show your recipes and logs, sync them, display what you chose to share, and keep the community safe by reviewing reports. I don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

I don't share your information with anyone except the service providers above (Apple, Supabase) that store it for the app to work, and when the law requires it. Content you make public is visible to everyone who uses the app or visits nightcap.me.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete Account. This deletes your cocktails, logs, favorites, bar, and tags from your device and your iCloud; removes everything you shared from Community; and removes cocktails you added to the library. It cannot be undone. Reports you filed may be kept so I can keep the community safe. You can also email me to ask for deletion, and you can stop the app using Sign in with Apple at any time in Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

Safety and content

You can report cocktails and cheers and block people from within the app. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and content that breaks the Terms of Use is removed. People who repeatedly break them can be removed.

Age

The app is about alcoholic drinks and is intended for adults of legal drinking age in their country. It isn't directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update this page and the date above.
