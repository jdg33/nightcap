Good Measure support

A cocktail app: browse hundreds of recipes, build your own, keep a log of what you've had.

Need a hand? Email jesse.d.goldstein@gmail.com. I read everything, and reports about abusive content are looked at within 24 hours.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Account → Delete Account. It permanently removes your cocktails, logs, favorites, bar, and tags, and everything you shared. No email needed.

How do I report something or block someone?

Tap the "…" menu on a Community cocktail, a cheer, or a drink page and choose Report or Block this person. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Profile → Support & safety lets you reset anyone you've blocked.

A cocktail I shared isn't showing up

You need to be signed in to iCloud and online. The app retries failed shares the next time you open it. New cocktails can take a little while to appear on nightcap.me.

My cocktails aren't syncing between devices

Check that both devices use the same Apple ID, are signed in to iCloud, and have iCloud turned on for Good Measure in Settings → your name → iCloud.

Why does the app say "iCloud needed"?

Community and sync use your iCloud account. Sign in to iCloud in Settings to use them. The cocktail library works without it.
